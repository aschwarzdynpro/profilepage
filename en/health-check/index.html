Legacy customizations, slow forms, failing flows, unclear permissions. Most systems run, but nobody knows exactly how stable they are. The health check brings clarity before risks turn into outages.

What gets checked

Eight dimensions, each with a guiding question that has an answer by the end.

01

Solution architecture and layering

Are solutions split sensibly, publishers consistent, layers under control?

02

Customization quality

How much legacy, unused and overloaded configuration sits in tables, forms and views?

03

Code quality

Are plugins, JavaScript and PCF maintainable, up to date and free of Solution Checker findings?

04

Automation

Do flows and workflows run reliably, with clear owners and without outdated technology?

05

Performance

Where do queries, synchronous logic or API limits slow your users down?

06

Data and capacity

How are storage, data quality and growth developing?

07

Security and permissions (overview)

Are there obvious risks in roles, admin rights and access?

08

ALM and operations (overview)

Are deployments, environments, rollback and documentation fit for purpose?

Security, ALM, and licensing and capacity are assessed at overview level. Each of them has its own report for an in-depth analysis. Implementing the actions is not included. Prerequisites and what is out of scope are listed at the end of the page.

How it runs

Four sessions over five days, with the tool-supported analysis in between. The workshop afterwards is optional.

  1. Kickoff (90 minutes)

    Clarify the current situation, pain points, goals and access

  2. Tool-supported analysis with read access

    Review of your environments with my own analysis toolset

  3. Two to three short interviews

    With development, makers and operations, 45 minutes each

  4. Results presentation (1 hour)

    We walk through the scorecard, risks and action plan together

  5. Workshop as neededoptional

    We talk through the recommendations at a conceptual level: how to slice the actions, in what order, and who takes on what. We agree on scope and effort beforehand; it is not included in the fixed price.

In total, your team spends about 4 to 5 hours on the health check. Nothing in your environments is changed.
