Legacy customizations, slow forms, failing flows, unclear permissions. Most systems run, but nobody knows exactly how stable they are. The health check brings clarity before risks turn into outages.

Scope: Dynamics 365 CE and Power Platform

What gets checked

Eight dimensions, plus the code analysis where the environment provides it. Each with a guiding question that has an answer by the end.

01

Capacity and licenses

How are storage, the audit log and growth developing, and where do purchased licenses sit unused?

02

Performance and operations

Where do queries, overloaded forms, synchronous logic or throttling slow your users down?

03

Security

Who is actually allowed to do what: roles, administrators, organization-wide access and assignments?

04

ALM and customization

Are solutions split sensibly, publishers consistent, layers under control and Solution Checker findings dealt with?

05

Automation

Do flows and workflows run reliably, owned by service accounts and without outdated technology?

06

Data model and data quality

How much legacy sits in tables and forms, and are duplicates, mandatory fields and fill rates under control?

07

Usage and adoption

Who actually works in the system, and which licenses and features stay unused?

08

Environments and governance

Are the environment landscape, DLP policies, backups and the platform version fit for purpose?

09

Code and extensions where available

Is your JavaScript maintainable, free of deprecated APIs and without hard-coded environment URLs or secrets in the code?

The ninth dimension requires that your JavaScript, plugins or a repository can be analyzed; in environments that have grown over years that is almost always the case. Integration (Dual Write) is added where links are running. The specialist reports on the security model, performance and form fill rates, which the rating is based on, come with the results. Implementing the actions is not included. Prerequisites and what is out of scope are listed at the end of the page.

    Clarify the current situation, pain points, goals and access

  2. Tool-supported analysis with read access

    Review of your environments with my own analysis toolset

  3. Two to three short interviews

    With development, makers and operations, 45 minutes each

  4. Results presentation (1 hour)

    We walk through the scorecard, risks and action plan together

  5. Workshop as neededoptional

    We talk through the recommendations at a conceptual level: how to slice the actions, in what order, and who takes on what. We agree on scope and effort beforehand; it is not included in the fixed price.

In total, your team spends about 4 to 5 hours on the health check. Nothing in your environments is changed.

Frequently asked questions about the Dynamics 365 health check

Price, duration, access and limits

The answers are spread across this page, collected here.

What does a Dynamics 365 health check cost?

€5,000 plus VAT, fixed scope, with no tiers and no variants. What this page describes is what the price covers. Implementing the actions is ordered separately.

How long does the health check take, and when can it start?

Five days. They cover the kickoff, the tool-supported analysis, two to three short interviews and the results presentation. The start is usually two to three weeks after you place the order, and delivery is remote.

How much time does my team need to set aside?

About 4 to 5 hours in total: 90 minutes for the kickoff, two to three interviews of 45 minutes each, and one hour for the results presentation. Plus one fixed contact for questions in between.

What access do you need, and is anything changed in my environments?

Read access to the production Dataverse environment and one upstream environment, to the Power Platform admin center and, if you use them, to Azure DevOps or GitHub. Nothing is changed: the analysis reads, it does not write.

What do I get at the end?

Three documents: a health scorecard across eight dimensions and the code analysis, each with a traffic-light rating and the reasoning, the ten biggest risks with impact and affected environments, and an action catalog in three tiers along the roadmap, namely 30, 60 and 90 days. Plus the specialist reports on the security model, performance and form fill rates. The documents are yours.

Is implementing the actions included?

No. The health check ends with the action catalog. You implement it in-house, with your current partner or with me. The optional workshop, where we talk through how to slice the actions and in what order, is not included in the fixed price either.

Which systems does the health check cover?

Dynamics 365 Customer Engagement, meaning Sales, Customer Service and Field Service, and the Power Platform on Dataverse. Finance & Operations itself and integrations outside the Power Platform are out of scope. For Dual Write, the Dataverse side is read, the Finance & Operations side is not.

Is the health check worth it if the system is running?

That is what it is for. Most systems run, but nobody knows exactly how stable they are. It covers capacity and licenses, performance and operations, security, ALM and customization, automation, data model and data quality, usage and adoption, and environments and governance, plus code and extensions where available. Findings are measured, not estimated.
