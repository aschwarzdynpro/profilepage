Audit-proof archiving and deletion for Dataverse

Situation

Retention obligations under GoBD and deletion deadlines under GDPR overlap on the same records. Native Dataverse long-term retention was not an option: forced cascades onto child tables, no field exclusions, no review window before deletion.

Solution

A custom, configurable rule engine for retention, archiving and deletion directly on Dataverse. Every record is archived before its status changes, and the rules live in configuration rather than in code.

Outcome

Archive and deletion dates are set once and cannot be changed afterwards. Auditors can retrieve any individual record by reference. The business side controls the rules through configuration, without touching code.

Technical implementation

Nine configuration tables: retention classes, guard conditions that can be checked one by one, a matrix for polymorphic activity references, field exclusions. Three scheduled jobs (evaluation, archiving, deletion) in .NET 8 write an archive of each record to immutable blob storage before they change its status. Stack: Dataverse, .NET 8, Data Lake Gen2, Synapse Link, Azure DevOps.

Dynamics 365 in phases: from Sales in Germany to rollouts in Switzerland and France

Situation

Sales, customer service and field service were to move onto a shared Dynamics 365 platform, starting in Germany and later in other national subsidiaries. Each phase had to build on the previous one without reworking it.

Solution

One target architecture for all phases from the start: Sales in Germany first, then Customer Service and Field Service, followed by the rollouts to Switzerland and France. Along with that, standardized ALM, so that every phase is delivered reproducibly on the same foundation.

Outcome

New modules and countries were added on the same architecture without reworking existing processes. Releases run through pipelines instead of manual exports.

Technical implementation

Extended SharePoint integration, automatic assignment of sales territories, an offline-capable Canvas App for capturing leads at trade fairs and a field service dashboard built as a Code App. Extensions as C# plugins and PCF controls. Contact Center with WhatsApp and email, including automatic case matching

Situation

A high volume of customer emails about bookings, rebookings and complaints in shared mailboxes. Agents looked up the matching case by hand, and deadlines before departure were not prioritized. WhatsApp was missing as a channel entirely.

Solution

Analysis and design of the contact center solution, setup of ALM on Azure DevOps and leading the implementation of the WhatsApp and email channels. Incoming messages are matched to the case automatically and prioritized by urgency.

Outcome

Most emails reach the right case without anyone searching by hand. Urgent cases land at the top of the queue automatically, and WhatsApp runs through the same routing.

Technical implementation

Dynamics 365 Contact Center with Omnichannel for WhatsApp and email via Azure Communication Services. A rule set and flows match emails to the booking based on booking numbers and content, and prioritize them by SLA depending on how close the travel date is. ALM on Azure DevOps.

How I work

Dipl.-Inf. (FH), working with Microsoft CRM since 2007. 15 years as an employed developer, consultant and architect, freelance under the DynamicsPro brand since 2022, embedded in client teams or as a subcontractor for Microsoft partners.

I design the architecture, steer the implementation and am accountable for it. I work closely with the development team and get hands-on myself when needed. I explain architecture decisions to stakeholders and project sponsors as well as to the developers. Four principles that have held up over the years:

System of record first, then the interface

Prices belong in the ERP, personnel data in the HR system, customers in one defined place. Settle that at the start and you save yourself the later argument over which copy is right.

AI with a realistic plan

Copilot and agents pay off where the data and processes are ready for them. I check up front which scenarios hold up, what they cost in day-to-day operation and which prerequisites are missing, and I introduce AI in stages.

Compliance is part of the architecture

GDPR, GoBD (German bookkeeping rules) and co-determination under Section 87 of the German Works Constitution Act are not side issues in the DACH region. I plan their requirements into the architecture from the start, for example deletion periods, logging and permissions, instead of retrofitting them at the end.

Handover is part of the job

Every solution comes with a pipeline, documentation and a team that understands it. I have no interest in staying indispensable.
